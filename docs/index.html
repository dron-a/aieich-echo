Taxila WhatsApp Sync

Link your Taxila account in 3 steps to receive automated deadline and quiz reminders on WhatsApp.

  1. Drag this green button up into your browser's Bookmarks Bar:
    ⚡ Taxila Sync
    If your bookmarks bar is hidden, press Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows).
  2. Open your Taxila Profile and click "Log in to the mobile app" so the QR code appears.
  3. Click the ⚡ Taxila Sync bookmark in your bar. A card will pop up allowing you to either click "Open WhatsApp" or copy the code.
